Privacy Policy

For the Shark Android app
Package name: com.shark.shark
Last updated: 31 October 2026
Developer / publisher: AhmedRz1122

This Privacy Policy explains how the Shark mobile application (“Shark”, “the app”, “we”, “us”) accesses, collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use Shark on Android. It is written to support Google Play requirements, including the Data safety form and disclosures for advertising and sensitive permissions.

Shark is designed so that core productivity features (text capture, OCR, documents, scanner, local history) run on your device. Shark does not create a Shark user account or Shark cloud profile for your content.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the Shark app published by AhmedRz1122 on Google Play and distributed under package name com.shark.shark. By installing or using Shark, you agree to the practices described here.

3. Information Shark accesses or processes

Depending on the features you use, Shark may access:

  • Screen content — with your explicit MediaProjection / screen-capture permission, to read text from other apps (Float Grab / overlay tools). Shark can show a floating control over other apps when you enable that feature.
  • Camera and photos / media — when you choose camera, gallery, document scan, QR / open-with, or share images and files into Shark.
  • Microphone — for on-device voice typing while editing text when you enable it. A fuller voice-agent experience may be marked “coming soon” in the app.
  • Files you open or share — documents, images, video, and other files you select, open with Shark, export from scans, or publish for local Wi‑Fi sharing.
  • Network / Wi‑Fi state — Shark requires an internet connection to use the app; connectivity is also used for ads and same‑network file sharing.
  • Notifications — when needed for foreground screen-capture or related system services.
  • Device advertising identifiers — used by Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to show personalized or non-personalized ads according to your device settings and Google’s policies.

4. What stays on your phone

Text recognition (OCR), document tools, saved text catches (“catch log”), opened-file history, theme preference, and (when available) voice shortcuts are processed and stored locally on your device. Shark does not create a Shark cloud account, and your photos and extracted text are not uploaded to a Shark-operated cloud service.

Local Wi‑Fi share only works on the same network as your phone. Only files you explicitly publish can be downloaded by others who scan your Shark QR code or open the share link. You can remove a published file at any time to stop sharing it (up to the in-app share limit).

5. Internet, third parties, and advertising

A working internet connection is required to use Shark. Features you initiate (for example opening a web search, starting a media download from a URL you provide, or loading ads) contact third-party services.

Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)

Shark may show banner ads and occasional full-screen (interstitial) ads after you finish a natural break in a task (such as a download, text catch, document export, or scan share), using Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Ad partners may collect or receive device and usage information as described in:

  • Google’s Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data from apps that use their services (AdMob)
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

You can limit ad personalization in your Android settings (Google → Ads) and review Google’s ad settings at adssettings.google.com.

On-device Google ML Kit

On-device Google ML Kit libraries may be used for text recognition and document scanning. Processing for those features is intended to run on your device.

6. How we use information

  • To provide features you request (capture, OCR, documents, scanner, share, history, settings).
  • To keep the app online-gated and functional.
  • To display ads and measure ad performance via AdMob.
  • To improve reliability on your device (Shark does not require a separate analytics account).

We do not sell your personal information.

7. Data retention and deletion

Data stored by Shark on your device (such as saved text history, opened-file history, and preferences) remains until you delete it in the app (for example Clear text history or Storage tools), clear Shark’s app data in Android settings, or uninstall Shark.

Because Shark does not offer cloud account registration, there is no separate Shark cloud profile to delete. If you use third-party services through the app (search, downloads, ads), their own retention rules apply.

8. Permissions

Android runtime or special permissions (screen capture, display over other apps, camera, microphone, notifications, media/storage, nearby Wi‑Fi devices where applicable) are requested only when needed for a feature you use. You can deny or revoke permissions in system settings; related features may stop working.

Screen capture and overlay are sensitive capabilities. Shark uses them only after you grant permission and only to provide Grab / Float Grab text tools you start. You can stop the floating session from the app or notification actions.

9. Children’s privacy

Shark is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through Shark in a way that concerns you, contact us using the details below and we will take appropriate steps.

10. Security

We aim to limit data leaving your device and to use platform protections for local storage and permissions. No method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure. You are responsible for keeping your device locked and for what you choose to share over Wi‑Fi share or external apps.

11. International users

Shark may be used in different countries. Where privacy laws apply (for example GDPR or similar frameworks), this policy describes our practices: on-device processing of your content, advertising via Google, and no Shark cloud account. For requests related to your local data, use in-app clear / delete options or uninstall.
