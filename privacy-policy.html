Privacy Policy

For the Shark Android app
Last updated: 18 October 2026
Developer: AhmedRz1122

This Privacy Policy explains how the Shark mobile application (“Shark”, “we”, “us”) accesses, collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the app. Shark is designed to keep most productivity features on your device.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the Shark app published by the developer AhmedRz1122. By using Shark, you agree to the practices described here.

2. Information Shark accesses or processes

Depending on the features you use, Shark may access:

  • Screen content — with your permission, to capture text from other apps (MediaProjection / screen capture) and optionally show an overlay floating control.
  • Camera and photos / media — when you choose camera, gallery, document scan, QR scan, or open shared images/files.
  • Microphone — for on-device voice typing / speech features when you enable them. Full voice-agent control may be marked coming soon in the app.
  • Files you open or share — documents, images, video, and other files you select, open with Shark, or publish for local Wi‑Fi sharing.
  • Network / Wi‑Fi state — to check connectivity (Shark requires an internet connection for use), serve ads, and run same‑network file sharing.
  • Notifications — when needed for foreground screen-capture or related system services.
  • Device advertising identifiers — used by Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to show ads, subject to your device ad settings and Google’s policies.

3. What stays on your phone

Text recognition (OCR), document tools, saved text catches, theme preference, and (when available) voice shortcuts are processed and stored locally on your device. Shark does not create a Shark cloud account, and your photos and extracted text are not uploaded to a Shark cloud service.

Local Wi‑Fi share only works on the same network as your phone. Only files you explicitly publish can be downloaded by others who scan your Shark QR code or open the share link. You can remove a published file at any time to stop sharing it.

4. Internet, third parties, and advertising

A working internet connection is required to use Shark. Some features (for example YouTube-related downloads or web search you initiate) contact third-party services you choose to use.

Shark may show banner ads and occasional full-screen (interstitial) ads after you finish a task (such as a download, text catch, document export, or scan share), using Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Ad partners may collect or receive device and usage information as described in Google’s Privacy Policy and AdMob guidance.

On-device Google ML Kit libraries may be used for text recognition and document scanning. Processing for those features is intended to run on your device.

5. How we use information

  • To provide app features you request (capture, OCR, documents, scanner, share, history, settings).
  • To keep the app online-gated and functional.
  • To display ads and measure ad performance via AdMob.
  • To improve reliability and fix issues based on how the app behaves on your device (no Shark analytics account is required).

We do not sell your personal information.

6. Data retention and deletion

Data stored by Shark on your device (such as saved text history and preferences) remains until you delete it in the app (for example, Clear text history) or uninstall Shark / clear app data in Android settings. Uninstalling removes Shark’s local app storage on that device.

Shark does not offer cloud account registration. If you use third-party services through the app, their own retention rules apply.

7. Permissions

Android runtime or special permissions (screen capture, overlay, camera, microphone, notifications, media/storage, nearby Wi‑Fi devices where applicable) are requested only when needed for a feature. You can deny or revoke permissions in system settings; related features may stop working.

8. Children’s privacy

Shark is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Security

We aim to limit data leaving your device and to use platform protections for local storage and permissions. No method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of Shark after an update means you accept the revised policy.

11. Contact

For privacy questions about Shark, contact the developer on GitHub: https://github.com/AhmedRz1122

In-app summary: open Shark → Settings → Privacy.
