Privacy Policy

For the Shark Android app
Package name: com.shark.shark
Last updated: 6 November 2026
Developer / publisher: AhmedRz1122

This Privacy Policy explains how the Shark mobile application (“Shark”, “the app”, “we”, “us”) accesses, collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use Shark on Android.

Shark is designed so that core productivity features (text capture, OCR, documents, scanner, local history) run on your device.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the Shark app published by AhmedRz1122 on Google Play and distributed under package name com.shark.shark. By installing or using Shark, you agree to the practices described here.

2. Information Shark accesses or processes

Depending on the features you use, Shark may process content and device signals on your phone. With your permission, that can include screen content (for Grab / Float Grab text tools and overlay controls), camera or gallery media, documents and other files you open or share, microphone input for on-device voice typing, and notifications needed for foreground screen-capture services. Shark also uses network connectivity for ads and same-network Wi‑Fi file sharing.

Text recognition, document tools, catch history, opened-file history, theme preference, and related settings are processed and stored locally. Your photos and extracted text are not uploaded to a Shark-operated cloud service. Local Wi‑Fi share only works on the same network; only files you explicitly publish can be downloaded by others, and you can stop sharing at any time.

When ads are shown, Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) may process device and advertising-related data (such as the advertising ID) according to your device ad settings and Google’s policies. Features you initiate—such as opening a web search, starting a download from a URL you provide, or loading ads—contact third-party services over the network (HTTPS where supported).

3. Internet, third parties, and advertising

A working internet connection is required to use Shark.

Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)

Shark may show banner ads and occasional full-screen (interstitial) ads after you finish a natural break in a task (such as a download, text catch, document export, or scan share), using Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Ad partners may collect or receive device and usage information as described in Google’s Privacy Policy and How Google uses data from apps that use their services (AdMob).

You can limit ad personalization in your Android settings (Google → Ads) and at adssettings.google.com.

On-device Google ML Kit

On-device Google ML Kit libraries may be used for text recognition and document scanning. Processing for those features is intended to run on your device.

4. How we use information

  • To provide features you request (capture, OCR, documents, scanner, share, history, settings).
  • To keep the app online-gated and functional.
  • To display ads and measure ad performance via AdMob.
  • To improve reliability on your device (Shark does not require a separate analytics account).

We do not sell your personal information.

5. Data retention and deletion

Shark does not create a Shark user account or Shark cloud profile. Data stored on your device (such as saved text history, opened-file history, and preferences) remains until you delete it in the app (for example Clear text history or Storage tools), clear Shark’s app data in Android settings, or uninstall Shark. There is no separate Shark cloud profile to delete.

If you use third-party services through the app (search, downloads, ads), their own retention rules apply.

6. Permissions

Android runtime or special permissions (screen capture, display over other apps, camera, microphone, notifications, media/storage, nearby Wi‑Fi devices where applicable) are requested only when needed for a feature you use. You can deny or revoke permissions in system settings; related features may stop working.

Screen capture and overlay are sensitive capabilities. Shark uses them only after you grant permission and only to provide Grab / Float Grab text tools you start. You can stop the floating session from the app or notification actions.

7. Children’s privacy

Shark is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through Shark in a way that concerns you, contact us using the details below and we will take appropriate steps.

8. Security

We minimize data leaving your device and recommend that you keep your device locked and only share files with trusted individuals. No method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure.

9. International users

If you are located in the EEA/UK (or another region with similar rights), you may request access to or deletion of your local Shark data by using the in-app clear options, clearing app data, or uninstalling the app.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of Shark after an update means you accept the revised policy. The live version is always available at this page URL.
